Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital Citizenship is the responsible, ethical, and safe use of technology and the internet. It's about how people behave online and how they use digital tools in a way that respects themselves and others.

What is Digital law?

Digital law refers to the rules and regulations that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital content. It focuses on what is legal and illegal in online spaces.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy is a set of rules that explain how technology, internet, and digital resources can and cannot be used in a school, workplace, or organization.

What is Plagarism?

Plagiarism is when someone uses another person's work, ideas, or words without giving proper credit and presents them as their own.

What is acadeic dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any behavior that gives a student an unfair academic advantage by breaking school or class rules.

What are the rules for AI Generates Content?

AI-generated content must be used only when allowed, with transparency, and not submitted as entirely your own work. Users are responsible for verifying accuracy and following school or organizational policies.
