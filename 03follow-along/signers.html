Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress, famous for his large, bold signature.
Thomas Jefferson
Main author of the Declaration of Independence.
Benjamin Franklin
Inventor, diplomat, and key Founding Father.
John Adams
Strong advocate for independence and later became the 2nd President of the United States.
Samuel Adams
Leader of the American Revolution and political activist.
Roger Sherman
The only person to sign the Declaration of Independence, the Articles of Confederation, and the U.S. Constitution.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
One of the wealthiest signers and the only Catholic signer of the Declaration.

Each of these individuals played a significant role in the founding of the United States and is remembered for their courage in signing the Declaration, an act considered treasonous by British Crown.
