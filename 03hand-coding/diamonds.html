The Diamond Lab: Sustainable Innovation

At Stela Marea, we believe the future of jewelry is grown, not mined. Lab-grown diamonds provide the same physical, chemical, and optical properties as mined stones but with a significantly lower environmental impact on our coastal ecosystems.

The Science of Growth (Since 1954)

The journey of the lab-grown stone began in 1954 with General Electric's "Project Superpressure." This breakthrough proved that high-quality gemstones could be cultivated in a controlled environment. --SOURCE

  • Primary Growth Requirements:
    1. Pure Carbon Source (Graphite or Methane gas)
    2. Extreme Temperature (up to 1500°C)
    3. Intense Pressure (similar to the weight of the Eiffel Tower)
  • Timeline: A diamond takes 2–4 weeks to grow in the lab versus billions of years in the earth.

--SOURCE

Modern Creation Methods

  1. CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition): A process where diamonds are grown from a hydrocarbon gas mixture, ideal for creating high-quality jewelry stones.
  2. HPHT (High Pressure High Temperature): The original method that mimics the intense heat and pressure of the Earth's mantle.
