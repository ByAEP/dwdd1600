Terminology & Definitions

Stela
An upright stone slab or pillar, often bearing inscriptions or carvings, serving as a monument in ancient cultures.
Lost-wax Casting
A goldsmithing technique where a metal sculpture is cast from an original wax model, a method mastered by Mixtec artisans.
CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition)
A modern laboratory process used to grow high-quality diamonds from a hydrocarbon gas mixture.
