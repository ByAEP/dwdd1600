The Diamond Lab: Sustainable Innovation

At Stela Marea, we believe the future of jewelry is grown, not mined. Lab-grown diamonds provide the same physical properties as mined stones but with a lower environmental impact.

Creation Methods

Modern technology allows us to cultivate diamonds using two primary scientific processes:

  • Primary Growth Requirements:
    1. Pure Carbon Source
    2. Extreme Temperature (up to 1500°C)
    3. Intense Pressure
  • Timeline: A diamond takes 2–4 weeks to grow in the lab versus billions of years in the earth.

CVD vs. HPHT

CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition) is a process where diamonds are grown from a hydrocarbon gas mixture. HPHT (High Pressure High Temperature) is the original method that mimics the Earth's mantle.
